Never miss critical verification codes, bank updates, or emergency server alerts again. SMS & Message Forwarder is a lightweight, privacy-focused automation tool that monitors incoming notifications and seamlessly dispatches matching alerts to your chosen destination.

🚀 Supported Forwarding Channels:
• SMS & Messaging App: Forward directly to another mobile number via cellular SMS (using SmsManager) or prepare drafts in your default messaging app (Google Messages, Samsung Messages).
• Instant Push (ntfy.sh): Zero-setup, password-free push alerts delivered in real-time to your computer browser or mobile device.
• Telegram Bot: Instant forwarding directly into your personal chat or group.
• Email (SMTP): Direct email dispatch supporting SSL/TLS (port 465) and STARTTLS (port 587) with Gmail App Passwords, Outlook, or custom mail servers.
• Webhooks: Dispatches structured JSON payloads to Discord, Slack, Zapier, n8n, or self-hosted API endpoints.
• Multi-Channel Mode: Forward to all configured destinations simultaneously.

🎯 Popular Use Cases:
• Multi-Device Convenience: Receive 2FA login OTPs on your PC, laptop, or tablet while your phone is charging in another room.
• Travel & Dual-SIM: Leave your secondary phone or local SIM connected to WiFi at home and receive banking alerts abroad via Telegram, Email, or SMS.
• DevOps & Server Alerts: Route incoming SMS downtime alerts directly to a team Discord channel or Slack webhook.
• Family Sharing: Automatically forward shared utility bills or streaming verification codes to a household chat.

⚡ Smart Filtering & Control:
• Keyword Matching: Forward only messages containing specific phrases (e.g. "otp", "alert", "code", "balance").
• Sender Whitelist & Blacklist: Restrict forwarding to trusted contacts or filter out spam and marketing broadcasts.
• Deduplication Cache: Built-in 15-second window prevents duplicate dispatches from updating notifications.
• Local History Logs: Review forwarded alerts and delivery statuses stored securely on your device.

🔒 Privacy & Security Guarantee:
• 100% On-Device Processing: Filter rules and message evaluations run entirely locally.
• Direct Encrypted Dispatch: Matching messages are sent straight to your configured destinations over TLS/SSL.
• Zero Cloud Intermediaries: No analytics, tracking SDKs, or developer servers.
• Open Source: Fully free and open-source software under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPLv3).
